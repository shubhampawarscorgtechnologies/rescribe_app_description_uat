Screen 1

Main Screen

The nurse tells the app where they are working. Nothing else can be opened until this is set, because a bed number only means something inside a specific ward.

Element What it does Why it matters
Select Branch Choose the hospital branch or unit the nurse is assigned to. Nurses at multi-branch groups use one login across sites.
Select Building / Floor / Ward Narrows the selection down to the exact nursing station. Prevents a nurse from acting on another ward's beds by mistake.
Nursing Bed Display Dashboard Shows the selected ward's bed picture and opens the Bed Display screen. First read of the shift — how full is the ward, what needs attention.

Support tip: "I can't see my patients" is almost always a wrong branch or ward selection, not missing data. Ask the nurse to confirm the branch and ward shown at the top of the screen first.

Home app bar

Quick Scan — QR / barcode

The QR button on the home app bar scans a patient's wristband, file sticker or slip. It reads a UHID or a visit number — the server works out which — and opens a Patient Quick Actions sheet for that bed's patient, without scrolling the ward list to find them.

Element What it does Why it matters
QR button Scans the code and resolves it to the patient's current active visit. Opens one patient directly instead of hunting the ward list.
Patient header Photo, name, age, gender, UHID, visit number and bed. Confirm the right patient before acting.
Ward actions Facesheet, Medication, Orders List, New Order, Documents, Visit History — plus, when the patient's status allows: Transfer Out, Transfer Bed, Mark / Tentative / Cancel / Approve / Actual Discharge, Send for Billing, Patient Deceased, Doctor Visit Posting. The same actions as the Bed Display card, reached by scan.
Check-in gating A patient not yet checked in shows only Pending Check In; one awaiting transfer shows only Transfer In. Mirrors the Bed Display rule — clinical actions stay hidden until the patient is in an active bed state.

Online-only. The scan resolve is a live lookup, so the QR button is greyed out with a message when the network is out. If a scanned code has no open visit, the sheet says so instead of opening a half-empty list.

Bed Display Screen

The screen a nurse lives in during a shift. It is a live board of every bed in the selected ward, and it is the doorway to each patient's record.

Search finds a bed by bed number, UHID, or patient name. The status filter reduces the board to one kind of bed.

2.5 Orders — special nursing service orders

Separate from clinical orders in the Facesheet. These are the billable nursing services and consumables used at the bedside.

List Contains Nurse action
Existing Service Orders Nursing services already ordered for the patient. Review, attach to the visit, post for charging.
Consumable Service Orders Consumables issued at the bedside, with filters to narrow the list. Select items and quantities used, then submit so they reach billing.

Swipe the table sideways to read every column →

Why it matters commercially: anything a nurse consumes but does not record here is revenue the hospital never bills. This list is the capture point.

2.6 Medication

A direct medication list for the patient, reachable from the bed without going through the full Facesheet — for a quick "what is this patient on" check during a round.

2.7 Pen Mode

Paperless documentation. The nurse writes or annotates directly on a document with a stylus or finger, the way they would on a paper chart, and the app stores it against the patient's visit. Replaces scanning handwritten sheets back into the record.

  • Handwriting on forms — annotate the document exactly where the pen would go on paper.
  • Saved to the visit — the marked-up document is stored with the patient, viewable later.
  • Works offline — writing done without network is queued and uploaded when the device reconnects.

2.8 Change Bed Status

Moves a bed from one state to another, which is what keeps the ward board accurate for everyone else — admissions, billing, housekeeping. Available actions depend on the bed's current status and the hospital's configuration.

Change Bed Status — actions available in the build
CHANGE BED STATUS

├── Discharge flow
│   ├── Mark for Discharge        nurse flags patient as ready
│   ├── Tentative Discharge       expected date/time recorded
│   ├── Discharge Approved        clinical sign-off received
│   ├── Send for Billing          handed to billing desk
│   ├── Actual Discharge          patient has physically left

├── Transfer flow
│   ├── Transfer Out              release patient to another ward/bed

Ordering rule: the discharge steps run in sequence. A bed does not become Vacant straight from Occupied — it passes through discharge, then usually Housekeeping, then Vacant. If a nurse reports a "missing" option, check the bed's current status first; the app only offers the transitions that are legal from where the bed is now.

Screen 3

Menu Screen

Account and device housekeeping. Small screen, but it holds the two controls support asks about most: cache and pending offline data.

Menu — sub-tree
MENU
├── Change Password                 update login credentials on the device
├── Clear Cache                     drop stored copies, force fresh data
├── View Sync Pending Offline Data  what is saved locally, not yet on the server
└── Logout                          end session on this device

Swipe the panel sideways to read the full width →

Item What it does When to use it
Change Password Sets a new password for the logged-in nurse. Routine credential change, or after a shared-device concern.
Clear Cache Removes locally stored copies of ward and patient data so the next open pulls fresh from the server. Stale or wrong-looking data that a refresh alone doesn't fix.
View Sync Pending Offline Data Lists everything entered offline that has not reached the server yet. Before logging out, before clearing cache, or when a nurse says "I saved it but it's not showing".
Logout Ends the session on the device. End of shift, or handing the device to another nurse.

Important sequence — tell every nurse this one.

Check View Sync Pending Offline Data before using Clear Cache or Logout. If the list is not empty, the device still holds work that has not reached the server. Get back on network and let it sync first.

Behaviour

Working without network

Wards have dead spots. The app is built so a nurse can keep working through them.

  • Reading: ward board, bed lists and previously opened patient data stay viewable from the device's stored copy.
  • Writing: entries made offline are saved on the device and placed in a sync queue.
  • Recovering: when the device is back on network, queued items upload. The Menu screen shows anything still waiting.
  • The one risk: clearing cache or logging out while items are still pending. That is why the pending list exists and why it is checked first.
In practice

A typical shift, in order

The same features, arranged the way a nurse actually meets them. This sequence is what a demo or a training session should follow.

  1. Log in and set contextMain screen — select branch, then building, floor and ward.
  2. Read the wardBed Display — count dashboard first, then filter to Today's Admitted and Pending Check-In to find what changed.
  3. Round the patientsOpen a bed → Facesheet → record vitals on the Vital Sheet, log fluids in the Intake/Output chart.
  4. Action clinical ordersFacesheet → Orders — raise Medicine, Lab or Radiology orders; move existing orders forward as they are completed.
  5. Capture services and consumablesOrders — post nursing service orders and the consumables used, so nothing goes unbilled.
  6. Document by hand where neededPen Mode — write on the form directly instead of on paper.
  7. Keep the board trueChange Bed Status — discharges, transfers, handover, so the next shift and the admissions desk see reality.
  8. Close the shiftMenu — confirm no pending offline data, then log out.

Term Meaning

Category Bed class (General, Semi-Private, Private, ICU). Determines tariff.
Pen Mode Handwritten documentation captured directly in the app instead of on paper.
Consumable A billable item used at the bedside — syringes, dressings, disposables.
Sync Pending Data entered on the device that has not yet been accepted by the server.
Handover Formal transfer of responsibility for a bed from one nurse to the next.
